Our Heritage

15+ Years of Passion for Precision Vision

Founded in 2009, Eye Care Optical Shop was created with a clear objective: to combine clinical optometry with modern, fashion-forward eyewear under one roof. Over the past decade and a half, we have grown from a modest neighborhood optician to one of Maharashtra's most trusted destinations for comprehensive eye examinations and branded spectacle dispensing.

We believe that wearing eyeglasses is not merely a medical necessity—it is an intimate reflection of your personality. Whether you are an executive seeking ultra-light titanium minimalism, an athlete needing polarized UV impact protection, or a parent seeking unbreakable frames for your child, our certified opticians take the time to evaluate your pupillary distance, facial contours, and daily digital screen habits.
