Screen Fatigue Science

Combat Digital Eye Strain (DES)

LED monitors, laptops, and smartphones project high-frequency Blue-Violet light (400 - 455 nm). This light scatters easily inside the eye, forcing the ciliary focusing muscles into continuous spasm. The result is dry stinging eyes, blurred vision, frontal headaches, and broken melatonin sleep cycles.

Our Blue-Cut lenses incorporate molecular nano-absorbers directly into the lens monomer. They selectively block 98% of harsh high-energy blue rays while permitting beneficial blue-turquoise light that regulates alertness and mood.

The 20-20-20 Rule for Screen Workers: Every 20 minutes of screen work, look at an object 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds to relax your eye muscles.

  • ✓ Zero Power or Prescription: Available for people with 20/20 natural vision as well as those with power.
  • ✓ No Heavy Yellow Tint: Crystal clear aesthetic without altering web design color balance.
  • ✓ Ultra-Violet 420 Cut: Integrated UVA/UVB barrier.
  • ✓ Anti-Reflective Coating: Eliminates ghost reflections from overhead tube lights.
