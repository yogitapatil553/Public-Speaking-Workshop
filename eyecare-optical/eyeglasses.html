Matching Frames to Your Face Shape

Round Face

Rectangular, square, or geometric frames add angular contrast and elongate facial features.

Square Face

Round, oval, and thin metal wireframes soften strong jawlines and forehead angles.

Oval Face

Naturally balanced. Suits almost any shape—especially aviators, cat-eye, and bold acetates.

Heart Face

Bottom-heavy shapes, rimless minimalist frames, and light-colored round glasses balance broad foreheads.
