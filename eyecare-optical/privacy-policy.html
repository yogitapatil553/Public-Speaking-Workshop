Privacy Policy

Your privacy and prescription data confidentiality are our top priorities.

Home › Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Eye Care Optical Shop ("we," "us," or "our") respects the privacy of our visitors and patrons. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, store, and protect any information you submit via our informational website or in-store customer cards.

2. Information We Collect

When you interact with our website contact forms, WhatsApp links, or visit our optical store, we may collect:

  • Personal contact details: Full name, telephone/mobile number, email address.
  • Ophthalmic information: Eyeglass prescriptions, sphere, cylinder, axis, addition, and pupillary distance provided voluntarily for lens customization.
  • General enquiry details and preferred appointment dates.

3. Use of Information

The information we collect is strictly used to:

  • Respond to your eyewear inquiries and service consultations.
  • Ensure accurate optical lens edging matched to your prescription.
  • Notify you when your spectacles or contact lenses are ready for collection at our store.
  • Send optional seasonal promotion alerts if requested.

4. No Third-Party Selling or Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your personal or medical prescription information to third-party marketing companies. Data is exclusively accessed by certified optometry staff for fitting purposes.

5. WhatsApp Communications

When you click "Enquire on WhatsApp", communication occurs directly through WhatsApp's encrypted platform according to Meta's privacy protocols. You may opt out of receiving messages from us at any time by simply replying "STOP".
