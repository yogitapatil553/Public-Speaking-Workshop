Understanding Presbyopia (40+ Vision)

Around age 40, the crystalline lens inside the eye naturally loses elasticity. Holding newspapers at arm's length is the universal sign. Our opticians calculate your exact reading addition (+1.00D to +3.50D).

Foldable Pocket Convenience

Never forget your reading glasses again. Our ultra-slim telescopic designs fold into hard aluminum cases the size of a ballpoint pen—fitting into any shirt pocket or purse.

Distortion-Free Aspheric Surfaces

Cheap over-the-counter magnifying glasses create dizzying peripheral fishbowl distortion. Our reading glasses use optical grade aspheric curves for edge-to-edge clarity.
