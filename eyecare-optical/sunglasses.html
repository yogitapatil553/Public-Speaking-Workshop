Eye Health Advice

Why Choose Polarized Lenses?

Standard dark sunglasses merely dim ambient brightness, causing pupils to dilate and potentially absorb more side UV rays if not UV400 certified. In contrast, polarized lenses contain a microscopic chemical filter that blocks horizontally reflecting glare from wet roads, car windshields, and water surfaces.

  • ✓ Safer Daytime Driving: Eliminates road asphalt mirage reflection.
  • ✓ Rich Color Contrast: Enhances natural depth perception and landscapes.
  • ✓ Prescription Sunglasses: Any power can be fitted into our sunglasses collection.
