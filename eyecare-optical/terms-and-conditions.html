1. Informational Website Scope

This website is published by Eye Care Optical Shop for informational and customer enquiry purposes only. We do not provide direct e-commerce checkout, online payment gateway processing, or automated home parcel dispatch. All transactions, precision pupillary measurements, and final lens dispensations occur directly at our retail store or via personalized staff interaction.

2. Product Availability & Price Ranges

Product photographs, descriptions, and price estimates shown on this website represent active store inventory. However, inventory changes continuously. We advise contacting our store via WhatsApp or phone to confirm live availability of specific frame models before visiting.

3. Eye Testing & Prescriptions

Our computerized vision checks are conducted by certified optometrists. If you bring an external prescription from an ophthalmologist or hospital, we will fit lenses strictly matching that doctor's written power. In cases of sudden prescription changes, our optometrist will verify your vision before edging lenses.

4. Progressive Lens Adaptation Guarantee

We provide a 30-day adaptation guarantee on all premium progressive lenses. If you experience persistent difficulty getting used to the viewing corridors, we will re-verify the fitting parameters and replace your lenses with alternative progressive designs or separate reading and distance pairs without any labor charge.

5. Frame & Coating Warranties

Branded frames (Ray-Ban, Titan Eye+, Carrera, Oakley) include official manufacturer warranties against manufacturing defects (e.g. solder joint failure, plating peeling under normal wear). Accidental damage, dog bites, vehicle accidents, or rough mishandling are not covered under manufacturer warranty, though our workshop will assist with low-cost repair.

6. Customer Inquiries & Communications

By submitting a contact form or initiating a WhatsApp inquiry, you authorize Eye Care Optical Shop representatives to contact you via telephone call, SMS, or WhatsApp regarding your optical needs.
